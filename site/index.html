Biography

I’m Ádám Ficsór, also known as nopara73. I created and host Longevity World Cup, and I host Immortal Combat.

Previously, I created Wasabi Wallet and ZeroLink, co-founded zkSNACKs, and co-authored WabiSabi and Programming the Blockchain in C#.

Forbes Hungary named me to its 30 Under 30 list in 2020.

Thanks for coming to my TED talk.

Bitcoin privacy

zkSNACKs
I co-founded the company behind Wasabi Wallet in 2018, served as its early CTO, and later led research. I left in December 2023.
Wasabi Wallet
I created the Bitcoin privacy wallet and led its early development. Version 1.0 was released in October 2018.
ZeroLink
I created the Bitcoin fungibility framework in 2017.
Dumplings
Reproducible CoinJoin statistics from Bitcoin blockchain data, separating fresh coins from remixes and examining change and post-mix consolidation.
Sake
CoinJoin denomination and amount-decomposition experiments, measuring equal outputs, change, fees, and block-space efficiency.

More work and research, including TumbleBit, P2EP, and the Wasabi Research Club.
