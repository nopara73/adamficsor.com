Biography

I am a software developer, also known as nopara73. I host the Longevity World Cup, an open-source longevity competition with athlete profiles, biological-age calculations, and public leaderboards.

Much of my work has focused on Bitcoin privacy. I helped create Wasabi Wallet and co-authored ZeroLink and WabiSabi, two approaches to improving Bitcoin privacy with CoinJoin.

I write about Bitcoin, longevity, software, and games. You can find me on GitHub, X, and Medium. My email is adam.ficsor73@gmail.com.
