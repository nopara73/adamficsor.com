Biography

I am Ádám Ficsór, also known as nopara73. I created Wasabi Wallet and ZeroLink, and co-founded zkSNACKs, the company behind Wasabi.

I co-authored WabiSabi and Programming the Blockchain in C#.

I created the Longevity World Cup, a competition to reverse biological aging, and host the Immortal Combat podcast.

Thanks for coming to my TED talk.
