Biography

I’m also known as nopara73. I created and host Longevity World Cup, a competition scored using blood-test estimates of biological age. I also host Immortal Combat, a podcast about longevity.

Previously, I worked on Bitcoin privacy. I created Wasabi Wallet and the ZeroLink privacy framework it originally used. In 2018, I co-founded zkSNACKs, the company that developed Wasabi. I was its early CTO and later led research, leaving in December 2023.

In 2020, I was named to Forbes Hungary’s 30 Under 30.

Bitcoin privacy and research

Much of my Bitcoin work was on CoinJoin: combining several people’s coins in one transaction to make ownership harder to trace.

ZeroLink
The framework I created in 2017 covers both the mixing protocol and how a wallet handles coins before and after a mix.
NTumbleBit
I worked with Nicolas Dorier on an implementation of TumbleBit, a protocol for private Bitcoin payments. I also wrote the five-part Understanding TumbleBit series.
Pay To EndPoint (P2EP)
A payment design I co-proposed in 2018. Both sender and receiver contribute coins, making ownership harder to infer.
Wasabi Research Club
I helped start these public discussions of Bitcoin privacy papers, which led into our WabiSabi research.
Dumplings and Sake
I built Dumplings to study CoinJoin transactions and started Sake to test how wallets choose the amounts of their mixed coins.

More work and research, including early wallet libraries, network privacy, and ShareLock.
